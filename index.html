Headphones or speaker? Wired headphones give the tightest follow, roughly 30–60 ms behind your voice. On the phone speaker, the app switches on echo cancellation so it doesn't chase its own sound; that adds a little delay and works best with the phone a short distance away and the instrument volume moderate. Avoid Bluetooth earbuds or speakers: they add 150–250 ms, which turns accompaniment into an echo.
